ABUTRON / LICENSES

1-day trial & licenses

Your one-time 24-hour trial starts only when your first DEMO broker account is actually authenticated and marked CONNECTED. Email verification and pending account linking do not start the countdown.

After the trial expires, new trades require a paid license. You can request a license here; payment is arranged outside ABUTRON and activated manually by Superadmin.

Your subscription pays for ABUTRON software and the listed broker-account seats ONLY — it does not fund any trading account.

IMPORTANT — LICENSE FEE IS NOT TRADING CAPITAL. ABUTRON sells software access only. No account equity, trading deposit, funded account or guaranteed profits are included. For LIVE trading, deposit your own funds directly into your own account with an approved broker (Deriv, IC Markets or FP Markets). DEMO uses virtual funds. ABUTRON does not receive your broker trading deposit. No card entry or automatic checkout is available; requesting a license does not activate trading.

ABUTRON / ACCOUNT-SPECIFIC EXECUTION

Automatic trading — independent ON / OFF

Each MT5 account has its own control. All accounts start OFF. A broker verification request is not a connected account; its ON switch stays unavailable until the MT5 execution gateway is working.

Loading independent controls…
A CRM ON switch does not prove a broker order was accepted, filled or protected. Check current broker-native execution receipts and stop modifications separately. Switching one account never activates another. LIVE authorization is separate from DEMO. An OFF status does not close existing broker positions.
